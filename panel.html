Mode

The archive restores links, not unsaved text, navigation history or scroll position. Silent video calls may also go undetected.

Exclude editors and meeting sites before enabling this mode. Automatic closing starts after a one-hour delay.

When a visit qualifies as attention

Quick scans do not make a tab more important. Separate short visits never add up to a qualifying visit.

When to suggest a tab

Time since the last qualified visit. Tabs with more total qualified attention are kept longer. Brief scans appear in statistics but do not reset the clock.
